# Imali-smart
  Imali Smart was inspired by the financial challenges faced by students, households, small business owners and informal traders, particularly those who struggle to manage money, plan budgets, save consistently and make informed financial decisions. We recognised the need for a simple, accessible and inclusive platform that helps people understand their finances, build financial confidence and improve their financial well-being. Our motivation is to use technology to make financial management easier by bringing personal finance tracking, business financial management, financial education and AI-powered guidance together in one platform. Through Imali Smart, we aim to empower individuals and small businesses to make smarter financial decisions, become more financially organised and work towards a more secure financial future.
